Community Chai
Community building over FOOD
Monthly community evenings with the aim to foster cross-pollination and connection across culinary, artistic, musical, academic and activist circles
Homebase at our fav community space 90mil. We sometimes venture out at different venues for fun
Open to collaborating on food-forward events, if food is core to your concept, not a side note, we want to hear from you.
Upcoming

We're collaborating with Torhaus Koch Kollektiv, merging our monthly with their monthly Küfa [Communal cooking with rescued food, open to all]

Starting as usual at 16:00 with cooking, we go live on air as Radio ToKoKo at 18:00, hosted by Aarti, who is part of both collectives. She will play revolutionary songs, sounds of protest and activism and interview two guests from India:

Deborshi Chakraborthy, scholar and guest speaker, on How India's Youth Uprising Is Cracking Modi's Electoral Autocracy.

Arshi Javaid, Berlin-based Kashmiri scholar and author/editor of Yaadgah: Memories of Srinagar, an anthology of essays and images tracing memory, loss and everyday life in Srinagar's old city.

Join us for an afternoon that blends chai, community radio, food-activism, and good company.

Come to Torhaus, 16-20h or tune in to THF Radio 18-20h
